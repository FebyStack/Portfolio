Positioning

I design the interface, write the code, and manage the social media after launch.

One practice, three jobs, AI in the loop. The promise is continuity: the idea a client approves is the one that ships and the one people see.

Who it is for
Clients who want one person to design it, build it and run its social. Teams hiring a designer who codes.
Proof
PilaPass for design and code. The Sev Clinic for operations and social. INKWILD for motion and AI.
Voice
Direct, plain, specific. Names the tool and the result. No hype, no borrowed titles.
